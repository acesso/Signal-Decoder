S3 Bridge Board

The new floor of the bridge stack: an ESP32-S3 with a WM8782 for 96 kHz I/Q, a PCM1808 for RX audio and a PCM5102A for TX audio, all clocked from one 24.576 MHz crystal. Hover or tap a wire to see where it goes; pick a part to see only its wires.

80 × 120 mm double-sided perfboard 2.54 mm grid AWG22 wiring I/Q 96 / 192 kHz · audio 48 kHz draft 6: status LCD on the edge (planned)

The two adapter boards

Both are the same double-sided board: a SOT-23-6 footprint on side A and a SOT-363 footprint on side B. Each side numbers the holes differently (side-A hole n is side-B hole 7 − n; the square hole is A1 = B6). The LP5907 is mounted side A up and the 74LVC1G157 side B (chip) up, as in the board drawing. The face that points down is drawn as seen through the board from above, so the square hole and every other hole sit in the same place on both panels of an adapter, and in the board drawing.
