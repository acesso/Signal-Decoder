FT8 coarse-search: WebGPU vs CPU/WASM feasibility benchmark

Loads a real 15s FT8 WAV, runs the coarse Costas-sync search 4 ways: (1) full GPU pipeline (9-dispatch FFT+correlation both on GPU), (2) GPU correlation-only (CPU-computed spectrogram fed to GPU), (3) plain-TS CPU reference (fft1920.ts + costasCorrelation.ts, no GPU), (4) FUSED GPU pipeline (all 9 FFT stages in ONE dispatch via workgroup-shared memory, no per-stage storage round-trip). Compares top-candidate correctness against ft8mon's own previously-measured output, and reports timings for each path.
